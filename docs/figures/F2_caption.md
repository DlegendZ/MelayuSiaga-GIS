# F2 — Recorded-event verification priority, Kampung Melayu

PPID 2025 only: RW 04/05 each have 25 records and 16 episodes (HIGH); RW 07/08 each
have three records and one episode (MEDIUM). Five other RW have NO_RECORD (≠ safe).
RW 04 and RW 05 share the same 25 source rows for **Jl. Kebon Pala II: RT 12–13 in
RW 04 and RT 10–11 in RW 05**. Episode counts must not be added across these RW;
the records do not establish inundation of entire RW polygons. Maximum recorded
depth is 300 cm in the four recorded units; this is a source maximum, not a spatially
uniform depth. All nine RW have UNKNOWN access and require field verification.

As context only, the Satu Data export with publisher periods 2019–2020 lists RW
04/05/07/08 in many 2020 months, RW 01–03/06 within January–February 2020, and no
explicit RW 09; its event dates are unverified and excluded from classification,
showing that NO_RECORD and one episode for RW 07/08 are limitations of the 2025
working set. This does not establish absence of flooding in RW 09.

Robustness: excluding five source rows labelled Genangan leaves RW 04/05 at 15
episodes each and HIGH. No class changes occur in nine threshold combinations
(REC_HIGH 2/3/4 × DEPTH_HIGH_CM 30/50/80, with REC_MED=2 and episode gap=2 days).
These checks assess class stability within this dataset, not completeness of records.
